### List of key foundational fuels
Key foundational fuels are listed below. The **primary fuel compounds** (shown in bold) are those found in the decomposition of the large hydrocarbons and real fuels, whereas the *secondary compounds* (shown in italics) are common fuels themselves. FFCM-2 attempts to describe the combustion of all of the listed C<sub>0-4</sub> fuels.

| $\text{C}_{0-1}$ species | $\text{C}_{2}$ species | $\text{C}_{3}$ species | $\text{C}_{4}$ species | Aromatics |
|:-------------:|:------------------:|:------:|:---:|:---:|
| $$\text{H}_2$$ **(hydrogen)** | $$\text{C}_2\text{H}_2$$ **(acetylene)** | $$p\text{-C}_3\text{H}_4$$ **(propyne)** | $$1,3\text{-C}_4\text{H}_6$$ **(1,3-butadiene)** | $$\text{C}_6\text{H}_6$$ **(benzene)** |
| $$\text{CO}$$ **(carbon monoxide)** | $$\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$$ **(ethylene)** | $$a\text{-C}_3\text{H}_4$$ **(allene)** | $$1\text{-C}_4\text{H}_8$$ **(1-butene)** | $$\text{C}_7\text{H}_8$$ **(toluene)** |
| $$\text{C}\text{H}_2\text{O}$$ **(formaldehyde)** | $$\text{C}_2\text{H}_6$$ **(ethane)** | $$\text{C}_3\text{H}_6$$ **(propene)** | $$2\text{-C}_4\text{H}_8$$ **(2-butene)** ||

{: .note }
In an earlier effort, FFCM-1 was developed for combustion of H<sub>2</sub>, CO, CH<sub>2</sub>O and CH<sub>4</sub>; the current FFCM-2 extends the coverage to all C<sub>0-4</sub> foundational fuels. The current release truncates at C<sub>4</sub> chemistry and does not consider aromatics (C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>6</sub> and C<sub>7</sub>H<sub>8</sub>).
